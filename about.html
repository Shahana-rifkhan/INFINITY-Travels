Our Story

Born from a Love of Travel

Infinity Travels was founded with a single dream-to make world-class travel accessible and unforgettable for every Sri Lankan. Since our establishment, we have dedicated ourselves to crafting perfectly curated holidays that go far beyond the ordinary.

From the backstreets of Ho Chi Minh City to the grand boulevards of Paris, our team has personally experienced every destination we offer. This insider knowledge means our clients receive more than just a package-they receive an authentic, extraordinary experience.

Over the years, we have grown into one of Sri Lanka's most trusted travel ateliers, serving thousands of satisfied travellers with bespoke itineraries, transparent pricing and unmatched personal care.
